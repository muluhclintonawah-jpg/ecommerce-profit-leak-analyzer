
# E-commerce Profit Leak Analyzer

**Understand where your online store is making money — and where profit is leaking.**

The E-commerce Profit Leak Analyzer is a practical spreadsheet-based tool designed to help online sellers examine profitability, discount impact, returns, cancellations, and product performance.

Built for use with **Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets**, the tool helps transform order records into useful business insights without requiring programming skills.

## The Business Problem

High sales do not always mean high profits.

Discounts, returned orders, cancelled transactions, and product costs can reduce the money a business actually earns.

This analyzer provides a structured way to investigate these factors and identify areas that may need attention.

## Key Features

- Discount impact and profitability analysis
- Returns and cancellations analysis
- Product and category performance
- Identification of potential profit leaks
- Monthly profitability trends
- Charts and plain-English findings

## How to Use

1. Open the spreadsheet in Excel or Google Sheets.
2. Enter or paste your order data into the designated input area.
3. Follow the included instructions.
4. Review the analysis, charts, and business findings.
5. Use the insights to investigate opportunities to improve profitability.
